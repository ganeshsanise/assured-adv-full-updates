Radio Advertising: The Fastest ROI You're Not Using

Published by Assured Advertising | 16 April 2026 | Blog

Every brand wants results. Fast, measurable, real-world results. And in a media landscape where the average digital display ad is ignored within 1.7 seconds, one channel continues to deliver - quietly, consistently and almost always underestimated.

Radio advertising.

According to Nielsen Audio research, radio reaches over 90% of adults on a weekly basis -more than any other medium including digital. And for every rupee invested in FM radio, brands typically see a return of ₹6 to ₹10 in incremental sales, depending on category and execution. That is not a niche result. It is a mainstream, repeatable return that most Indian brands are simply not capitalising on.

If your brand has not built radio into its media mix, you are likely leaving your fastest return on investment sitting unused.

Why Radio Still Works

In a world obsessed with digital metrics, radio has held its ground for one simple reason: it reaches people when no other medium can.

During the morning commute, in the kitchen, at the gym, at the shop - radio is on. Unlike your social feed competing for attention with fifteen other things simultaneously, a radio spot enters an environment of reduced distraction. The listener is not scrolling past it. It plays, and they hear it.

Research by Kantar Millward Brown found that radio generates a 49% higher brand recall lift than digital display advertising when used as part of a mixed media plan. That recall advantage is structural, not accidental. Frequency builds recognition, and radio’s listening patterns make high frequency both affordable and natural.

For businesses targeting regional or multilingual audiences across India, the advantage sharpens further. With stations like Red FM, Radio Mirchi, and Big FM broadcasting in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and more, across metros and Tier-2 cities, radio reaches audiences that digital campaigns frequently miss entirely.

The ROI Advantage Most Brands Overlook

Compared to television or large-format outdoor advertising, radio offers a significantly lower cost of entry without sacrificing reach. A strategically planned FM campaign running across three to four weeks can:

  • • Build frequency fast - a listener in the morning drive window alone can hear your spot four to six times across a single week, at a fraction of a comparable TV burst.
  • • Drive local, immediate action - radio consistently influences in-store footfall and direct enquiries within 24 to 48 hours of a spot airing, making it one of the fastest-response media available.
  • • Amplify existing media - when run alongside print, OOH, or digital, radio reinforces messaging and accelerates brand recognition. Adding radio to a digital campaign has been shown to increase that campaign’s overall effectiveness by up to 20%.
  • • Reach where digital cannot - in Tier-2 and Tier-3 markets where internet penetration is uneven and digital ad costs are rising; FM radio remains the most cost-efficient mass medium available.

The challenge is not whether radio works. The data is clear. The challenge is knowing how to use it for maximum efficiency, and that is where most brands either succeed or waste their budget entirely.

The Cost of Going in Without a Strategy

Many businesses approach radio advertising the way they approach classified ads - book a spot, run it, and hope it lands. The result is typically budget spent without measurable impact and a conclusion that “radio doesn’t work for us.”

What actually did not work was the approach. Radio ROI is deeply sensitive to execution: which station, which time band, what frequency, how the spot is written, whether it is reinforced by RJ mentions, and how it sits within the competitive landscape on that station at that time. A spot airing at 11am on a Tuesday reaches a fundamentally different audience than the same spot at 8am on a Monday. The difference in outcome can be significant.

Effective radio advertising is not just about running a spot. It is about being heard by the right people, at the right moment, often enough to matter, and tracking whether it worked.

The Multilingual Edge: Reaching India’s Real Audience

One of the most underestimated advantages of radio in the Indian market is language. India’s consumer base does not think, decide, or respond to advertising in a single tongue. A campaign built only in English or Hindi is a campaign that talks past a significant portion of its potential audience.

FM radio is uniquely positioned to close that gap. Regional stations broadcasting in Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, and Gujarati reach audiences at a level of cultural familiarity that no digital targeting algorithm fully replicates. A listener hearing a brand speak in their language, in their city, during their morning routine is not just reached, they are met.
