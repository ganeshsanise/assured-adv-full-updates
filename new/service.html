We Help Brands Make Noise

Our core expertise has always been making brands visible and effective. By offering four focused advertising services, we keep things simple and powerful.

  • Media Buying
  • Print Ads
  • Radio Ads
  • Television Ads
  • OOH Ads

Media Buying & Planning

We offer strategic media planning that maximizes visibility and budget across print, radio, and television, ensuring your message reaches the right audience at the right time.

Key deliverables include:

  • City-wise segmentation and demographic targeting
  • Frequency & scheduling optimization for maximum impact
  • Comprehensive budget planning and management
  • In-depth competitor tracking and analysis

Print Advertising

Reach readers through trusted editorial platforms in both newspapers and magazines, tailored for wide and niche audiences.

Newspaper Formats:

  • Display ads (Full, Half, Quarter page, Jackets)
  • Classifieds for job listings, real estate, and tenders
  • City-specific inserts and flyers
  • Publications: Times of India, Hindustan Times, Dainik Bhaskar, etc.

Magazine Placements:

  • Fashion, lifestyle, business, and inflight magazines
  • Premium ad spots: Inside covers, double spreads
  • Audience targeting by segment and geography
  • Distribution planning based on readership habits

Radio Advertising

Engage urban and regional audiences through captivating audio spots aired on top FM and local stations.

What we offer:

  • Customized radio spots in multiple languages
  • Strategic time-slot and program placement
  • RJ mentions, interviews, and live reads
  • FM partnerships with Red FM, Radio Mirchi, Big FM & more

Television Advertising

Showcase your brand through high-reach TV campaigns across regional and national channels, designed to build trust and impact.

Our TV ad services include:

  • Prime-time spots on GEC, news, and niche channels
  • Creative production of 10s/15s/30s commercials
  • Geo-targeted regional TV planning
  • Broadcast across platforms like Star, Sony, Zee, Sun, etc.

OOH Advertising

Make your brand unmissable with outdoor formats that dominate key urban and transit locations.

We manage the entire process:

  • Billboards, Metro, Airport & Mall Advertising
  • Bus Shelters, Unipoles, Pole Kiosks, Gantries
  • Strategic site selection based on footfall & traffic
  • Compliance, permits, and execution support
